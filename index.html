02 / Process

ONE CLAIM, END TO END

The system I am building, from the scanned document that starts a dispute to the letter that settles it.

Step 01 — Intake

A scanned Statement of Facts arrives

The port agent sends a PDF — often a photocopy of a fax. Every hour of laytime in the eventual claim traces back to this one document.

  • PDF
  • Multi-tenant intake

Step 02 — Extract

A vision-language model reads the port events

Events come back as editable, source-traceable suggestions rather than silent overwrites. Output is schema-validated, and the model falls back automatically if a call fails.

  • Gemini Vision
  • Schema validation
  • Model fallback

Step 03 — Compute

A deterministic rules engine applies the charter party

NOR validity, timezone-aware SHEX/SSHEX exclusions, weather and shifting exceptions, once on demurrage. Dependency-pure, so the same inputs always produce the same statement.

  • GENCON 94
  • ASBATANKVOY
  • Open source

Step 04 — Verify

Third-party evidence checks the counterparty

Weather archives, AIS vessel movement and sanctions screening test the claimed facts independently — so a line is backed by evidence, not just by the other side’s own SOF.

  • Weather archive
  • AIS
  • Sanctions screening

Step 05 — Claim

The claim drafts itself, with citations

Clause flagging by severity, time-bar tracking and sensitivity analysis feed an LLM-drafted letter that cites the source line behind every figure.

  • Time-bar tracking
  • Sensitivity analysis
  • Cited drafting
